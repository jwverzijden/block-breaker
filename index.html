⛏️ BLOCK BREAKER

Mine the whole field. Spend cubes on better gear. Dig deeper.

How to play

  • WASD / Arrows — move your miner around the field.
  • Click / hold — swing the pickaxe toward the cursor.
  • Break every block to clear the field and reach the next level.
  • Each block drops cubes; each level multiplies block HP and cube value.
  • Spend cubes in the shop (right side) on pickaxes, Fire and Lightning.
  • Your progress is saved automatically (and via the Save button).
